## 📌 Executive Summary

Every dataset referenced, curated, and benchmarked in **GuardShield AI** originates directly from top-tier peer-reviewed research papers published at flagship computer science conferences (**NeurIPS**, **ICLR**, **EMNLP**, **ACL**, **NAACL**). 

This expanded catalog contains **12 premier published benchmark datasets** divided into two operational categories:
1. **Pre-Scan Adversarial Defense**: Direct Prompt Injections, Automated Stealthy Jailbreaks, and Indirect Prompt Injections.
2. **Post-Scan Hallucination & Factuality**: Natural Language Inference (NLI) Grounding, Information-Seeking Dialogues, and Multi-Domain Knowledge Factuality.

## 📑 Detailed Paper Profiles for IEEE Research Paper

### 🛡️ Category A: Pre-Scan Prompt Injection & Adversarial Defense

#### 1. `JailbreakBench` (NeurIPS 2024)
- **Paper**: *JailbreakBench: An Open Robustness Benchmark for Jailbreaking Large Language Models*
- **Institution**: University of Pennsylvania (UPenn)
- **Contribution**: Standardized 100 distinct adversarial attack algorithms across 10 safety categories.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Source dataset for jailbreak patterns and adversarial prompt distribution.

#### 2. `HackAPrompt` (EMNLP 2023)
- **Paper**: *HackAPrompt: An Empirical Study of Prompt Injection Attacks on Large Language Models*
- **Institution**: University of Maryland (UMD) & Scale AI
- **Contribution**: Analyzed 600,000+ real-world prompt injection attempts across multiple LLMs.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Evaluates direct system prompt override defense.

#### 3. `AdvGLUE` (NeurIPS 2021)
- **Paper**: *Adversarial GLUE: A Multi-Task Benchmark for Robustness Evaluation of Language Models*
- **Institution**: University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) & Microsoft Research
- **Contribution**: Benchmark featuring 14 distinct NLP adversarial perturbation methods.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Validates model semantic robustness against word-level perturbations.

#### 4. `Do-Not-Answer` (NeurIPS 2023)
- **Paper**: *Do-Not-Answer: A Dataset for Evaluating Safeguards in Extreme Model Risk*
- **Institution**: Mohamed bin Zayed University of AI (MBZUAI) & University of Melbourne
- **Contribution**: Curated safety dataset of 936 prompt instructions across 5 risk areas and 12 harm categories.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Benchmarks non-compliance filtering on dangerous prompts.

#### 5. `AutoDAN` (ICLR 2024)
- **Paper**: *AutoDAN: Generating Stealthy Jailbreak Prompts on Aligned Large Language Models*
- **Institution**: University of Wisconsin-Madison & UC Davis
- **Contribution**: Automated genetic algorithm generating stealthy jailbreaks that bypass perplexity filters.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Adversarial testing of DeBERTa-v3 semantic disentangled attention.

#### 6. `BIPIA` (Microsoft Research 2023)
- **Paper**: *Benchmarking Indirect Prompt Injection Attacks on Large Language Models*
- **Institution**: Microsoft Research
- **Contribution**: First comprehensive benchmark on indirect prompt injection embedded within web/retrieved context.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Evaluates sidecar proxy performance on secondary input payloads.

### 🔍 Category B: Post-Scan Hallucination Detection & NLI Factual Grounding

#### 7. `BeaverTails` (NeurIPS 2023)
- **Paper**: *BeaverTails: Towards Improved Safety Alignment of LLM via Multi-Dimensional Preference Dataset*
- **Institution**: Peking University (PKU) & ETH Zürich
- **Contribution**: 330,000 human-annotated QA pairs balancing safety preference with helpfulness.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Serves as benign developer question baseline to calibrate precision (avoiding false alarms).

#### 8. `HaluEval` (EMNLP 2023)
- **Paper**: *HaluEval: A Large-Scale Hallucination Evaluation Benchmark for Large Language Models*
- **Institution**: Renmin University of China & Université de Montréal
- **Contribution**: 35,000 hallucinated responses spanning QA, dialogue, and summarization.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Benchmark for windowed Cross-Encoder NLI factual contradiction scoring.

#### 9. `TruthfulQA` (ACL 2022)
- **Paper**: *TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods*
- **Institution**: University of Oxford & OpenAI
- **Contribution**: 817 questions across 38 categories measuring whether LLMs mimic human superstitious falsehoods.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Benchmark for measuring token logit entropy spikes during factual uncertainty.

#### 10. `FEVER` (NAACL 2018)
- **Paper**: *FEVER: A Large-Scale Dataset for Fact Extraction and VERification*
- **Institution**: University of Cambridge & Amazon
- **Contribution**: 185,445 claims verified against Wikipedia evidence (Entailment, Contradiction, Neutral).
- **GuardShield Usage**: Foundation for training and calibrating the NLI Cross-Encoder verifier.

#### 11. `FaithDial` (ACL 2022)
- **Paper**: *FaithDial: A Faithful Benchmark for Information-Seeking Dialogue*
- **Institution**: McGill University, Mila & University of Alberta
- **Contribution**: 50,000 conversational dialogue turns annotated with token-level hallucinated spans.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Validates streaming token-level entropy spikes against human hallucination annotations.

#### 12. `KoLA` (ICLR 2024)
- **Paper**: *KoLA: Carefully Benchmarking World Knowledge of Large Language Models*
- **Institution**: Tsinghua University
- **Contribution**: Multi-level knowledge benchmark testing memorization, understanding, and factual hallucination rates.
- **GuardShield Usage**: Evaluates evolving factual drift across 19 knowledge domains.
